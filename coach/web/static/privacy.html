Privacy Policy

Coach · effective 29 May 2026

Coach is a personal AI training assistant that reads your Strava activity data to give you coaching feedback. This policy explains what data Coach handles, how it is used, and how you stay in control of it.

Coach does not store payment information, health records, or any data beyond what is listed above.

Read-only Strava access

Coach requests read-only permission (activity:read_all). It cannot create, edit, delete, or post anything to your Strava account — it can only read your activity history.

Who can see your data

  • You: you see your own activities and coaching chat history in the app, and no one else's.
  • The operator (whoever runs this Coach instance) can access your profile data, stored activities, and coaching chat messages via the database dashboard. The operator cannot see your LLM API key or Strava tokens in plaintext — these are encrypted in Supabase Vault and are never logged.

How your Strava data is used

Your Strava activity data is used solely to provide coaching context within Coach. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising. Specifically:

  • Activity data is passed to the AI model you have configured (e.g. OpenAI, Google Gemini, Anthropic Claude) only to generate coaching responses (inference) — never to train, fine-tune, or otherwise build AI or machine-learning models. This data leaves Coach and is processed by that third-party provider under their own terms of service and privacy policy.
  • The major providers do not train their models on data sent through their paid APIs. Both OpenAI and Anthropic state that inputs and outputs submitted via their APIs are not used for model training by default. Review the policy of whichever provider and API key you choose to supply.
  • Activity data is not shared with any other third party.
  • Activity data is not used to train models, build advertising profiles, or for any purpose other than generating your coaching responses.

This use of Strava data is in line with the Strava API Agreement, including its restriction on using athlete data to train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Retention and deleting your data

When you disconnect Coach from strava.com/settings/apps, Strava sends a deauthorization event to Coach. On receiving it, Coach immediately and atomically:

  1. Deletes your Strava tokens from Vault
  2. Deletes all stored activity data
  3. Clears the Strava connection from your account

This happens automatically — you do not need to take any action inside Coach, and you keep full control over your data.

Contact and support

For questions, data requests, or support, open an issue at github.com/t-ded/coach/issues. You can review or revoke Coach's Strava access any time at strava.com/settings/apps.
